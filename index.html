Trusted public references

Natural & holistic health, explored carefully.

A calm research library for whole-person health, complementary practices, movement, rest, food, herbs, supplements, and traditional systems—without miracle claims or product promotion.

01

Begin with the whole person

Health can include physical, emotional, social, behavioral, and environmental dimensions. The evidence for each practice still deserves its own careful review.

02

Use “natural” with care

Natural does not automatically mean harmless. Herbs, supplements, movement practices, and hands-on approaches can have risks, interactions, or limits.

03

Complement, do not conceal

Share supplements and complementary practices with a licensed clinician or pharmacist, especially during pregnancy, before surgery, or when using medicines.

How Aurora chooses a trusted public reference

Sources must be public, noncommercial, attributable to a recognized health or public-health institution, clear about evidence and uncertainty, and free of product sales. A listing is a research doorway—not an endorsement of every practice it discusses.

Important: Aurora is educational and reflective, not medical, psychological, psychiatric, or therapeutic treatment. Do not use these materials to diagnose yourself, replace prescribed care, or delay urgent or professional help. For questions about a practice, herb, or supplement, speak with a licensed health professional or pharmacist who knows your medicines and health history.
