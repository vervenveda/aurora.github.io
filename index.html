Aurora’s purpose

A humane place to restore, learn, and grow

Aurora is designed for small, voluntary practices: breathe for a moment, write a page, listen closely, notice progress, begin gently, or prepare for rest. It supports personal well-being while staying honest about the boundary between a self-guided wellness tool and professional health care.
